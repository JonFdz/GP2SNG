WASM Ogg/Vorbis encoder provenance

GP2SNG uses wasm-media-encoders@0.7.0 and only its Ogg Vorbis encoder path.
The Ogg WASM distributed by that package was built from:

- libogg 1.3.4, commit bada45718453ac27b56773ae663f7e65112f6a6e
- libvorbis 1.3.7, commit 0657aee69dec8508a0011f47f3b69d7538e9d262

Their full license texts are included as libogg.txt and libvorbis.txt.
wasm-media-encoders itself is MIT licensed and is covered by
NPM_RUNTIME_NOTICES.txt.

The upstream wasm-media-encoders package also contains an MP3/LAME encoder,
but GP2SNG does not intentionally use or distribute that encoder. Production
bundle validation requires the Ogg WASM payload to be present and the MP3/LAME
WASM payload to be absent.
